Company cards: four designs

Real companies and real numbers from Scout's database (10-25-26). Pictures and videos come from each company's own site, the App Store and YouTube. Every design has the same parts; only the layout changes. Each one is shown in the three places a card appears. "New" means a signal since your last visit (set to 10-18-26 here). Troveo isn't in Scout yet: it's here because two sources disagree on its raise.

Every card has

  • The product visual, with play and full screen in the bar under it
  • A Visit website button (or Search for website when none is found)
  • Funds Raised with its sources; "Not yet public" instead of Unknown
  • One why-it-fits paragraph, the latest signal and its date
  • Thesis fit (smaller), Track, votes, and More for the rest

How the visual is picked, first match wins

  • Product videoInternational Dance League (YouTube), Affinity Labs, ANORIA, Spash (their own files)
  • App Store screenshotsSleepSense
  • Website preview imagetash, Topdog
  • Logo plus Funds RaisedAxo Longevity, Troveo
