Toasts and Errors

Upper-right stack · green toasts fade · error cards stay until closed

Successes are two or three words with a check and fade (the older one is already dimming). Errors are never toasts: the card stays until its ✕, shows the whole message, a relative time, a repeat count, Copy Details, and a button that goes to the fix.
